# From screen sample to ray

Map a screen sample s to a unit u(s)∈TₚM. The ray is geodesic γₛ with γₛ(0)=p and γ̇ₛ(0)=u(s). Perspective, fisheye, and stereographic maps are camera policy, not geometry policy.

Euclidean γₛ(t)=p+t u(s) is not a general primitive. Eye should expose forward geodesic advance instead. Global exp inversion may have many answers in Nil, Sol, SL̃₂(ℝ), and S²×ℝ; a quotient object can appear in distinct directions.

**Invariant:** unit speed means g_{γ(t)}(γ̇,γ̇)=1. A frame-aware propagator must carry enough data to compare hit normals and light directions at the hit point.
