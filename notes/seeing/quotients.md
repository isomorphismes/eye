# Quotients: one world point, many lifted images

If Γ acts by isometries on X, quotient X/Γ identifies p with A(p). A fundamental domain is only a representative system. Crossing a wall updates the representative and all tangent data by dA.

A lifted object has Γ-translates. A ray in X may meet several lifts, showing multiple images of one quotient object. “Nearest object” needs a search region or distance-underestimator argument, not one representative.

The central paper’s §4 calls boundary bookkeeping teleporting. The quotient observer is continuous; coordinates change. Section 4.2 separately treats signed-distance functions. Eye should preserve that separation.
