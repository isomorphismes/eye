# Novello, da Silva, Velho, and Belolipetsky

**Status:** metadata-only.  
**Source:** Tiago Novello, Vinícius da Silva, Luiz Velho, Mikhail Belolipetsky, *How to See the Eight Thurston Geometries*, [arXiv:2005.12772](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12772).

This is a designated independent visualization source. It belongs beside the central ray-marching paper because it approaches the same family of spaces from a different rendering tradition. The central paper reports that Novello, da Silva, and Velho implemented in-space ray-tracing for all geometries except the product geometries, using Euler integration for SL̃₂(ℝ) and Sol. That report is not a substitute for inspecting their exact formulas or implementation.

This entry records a reading obligation: compare model choice, geodesic integration, observer control, quotient treatment, and numerical error claims against Coulon–Matsumoto–Segerman–Trettel. Until that comparison is written, no design choice in Eye depends on this source.
