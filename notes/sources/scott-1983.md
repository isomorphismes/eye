# Peter Scott, *The Geometries of 3-Manifolds*

**Citation:** Peter Scott, “The Geometries of 3-Manifolds,” *Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society* 15(5), 1983, 401–487. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/15.5.401).

Scott supplies the classification context for S³, E³, H³, S²×ℝ, H²×ℝ, Nil, Sol, and SL̃₂(ℝ). For Eye, “Thurston geometry” means more than a metric formula. It ties a simply connected model X to a transitive transformation group and to compact quotient manifolds.

The rendering problem has two layers. The model X supports local motion and geodesics. A discrete group Γ acting by isometries produces X/Γ. A representative can cross a fundamental-domain face while the quotient point remains continuous; dA transports its tangent data for A∈Γ.
