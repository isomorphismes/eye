# Discrete exterior calculus and Egison

**Status:** source-inspected at the repositories’ READMEs.

DiscreteExteriorCalculus.jl provides cell-complex and primal/dual mesh structures plus exterior derivative, Hodge star, codifferential, and Laplace–de Rham operators. This is useful for later field equations or discrete forms, not for immediate ray propagation. A cochain complex does not automatically provide a camera frame, smooth connection, or 3D quotient visibility.

Egison is a typed functional language with non-linear patterns, user-defined data types, and tensor-index notation. Its tensor notation may help readable symbolic experiments or pattern-rich algebraic structures. It is not necessary to parse Eye’s first Idriç type sketch. The shared lesson is narrow: surface notation can make indexed expressions readable; it does not settle a runtime representation.
