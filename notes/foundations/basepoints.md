# A tangent vector has a base point

A point p on a manifold is a location. A tangent vector v∈TₚM is a first-order way of leaving it. Coordinate triples can hide the difference but cannot remove it.

For M=ℝ², translation identifies tangent spaces canonically, so the same coordinate pair at p and at q can be compared. A sphere has no such translation. At the north pole an eastward vector lies in a different tangent plane from one at the equator. An ambient ℝ³ arrow can draw both, but that ambient identification is extra structure.

The tangent bundle is TM=Σ(p:M),TₚM. Eye’s ray state needs a point together with a velocity. A frame is more: an ordered basis of TₚM, often orthonormal for g. It makes “up,” “right,” and “forward” local facts.

**Check.** A coordinate change alters components by its Jacobian at p. A direction update that ignores p and the Jacobian cannot be coordinate-invariant.
