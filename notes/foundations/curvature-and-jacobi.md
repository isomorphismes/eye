# Curvature, Jacobi fields, and apparent separation

Curvature is R(X,Y)Z=∇ₓ∇ᵧZ−∇ᵧ∇ₓZ−∇_[X,Y]Z. With this convention a unit sphere has positive sectional curvature. Sources using the opposite convention negate R.

A Jacobi field J along γ measures separation of nearby geodesics:

D²J/dt²+R(J,γ̇)γ̇=0.

The differential of expₚ is encoded by Jacobi fields. Its determinant controls the local expansion of ray cones, hence the area density of geodesic spheres used in the central paper’s lighting model.

Conjugate points occur where d expₚ loses rank. A cut point ends unique distance minimization, perhaps before a conjugate point. Geodesic continuation remains possible, but “one target, one direction” fails.
