# The eight Thurston geometries

The central paper groups the eight geometries into isotropic models, products, and Lie-group-related geometries. Each entry records a model, metric or structural description, observer consequences, quotient cautions, and numerical boundary. These are research notes, not a claim that every formula has already been implemented in Eye.

| S³ | [sphere](s3.md) |
