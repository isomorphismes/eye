# SL̃₂(ℝ) — universal cover of SL₂(ℝ)

**Model.** The central paper §10 constructs SL₂(ℝ) as a circle bundle over H² through the adjoint action on its Lie algebra, then passes to a universal cover modeled topologically by H²×ℝ. The metric is not the ordinary product metric: fiber and horizontal geometry are coupled by the chosen homogeneous structure.

**Observer consequence.** This geometry is neither isotropic nor a product. It has a horizontal H²-like projection, a vertical fiber coordinate, and geodesic regimes depending on the balance of horizontal and fiber initial velocity. Using “H²×ℝ” as a storage type must not silently replace the SL̃₂ metric with the product metric.

**Propagation and transport.** The paper first integrates geodesic flow in SL₂(ℝ) through group data, then lifts it to the universal cover (§§10.2–10.3). It also carries a parallel-transport matrix. This is the right evidence boundary for Eye: exact implementation exists upstream, but its formulas require source-by-source extraction before reuse.

**Quotients.** Principal examples are related to unit tangent bundles of hyperbolic surfaces and orbifolds. The paper constructs a lifted lattice and a fundamental-domain strategy in §10.9.

**Numerical boundary.** Track fiber lift information; a projection to SL₂(ℝ) alone loses universal-cover position.
