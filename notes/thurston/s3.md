# S³ — positive constant curvature

**Model and metric.** Take the unit sphere S³⊂ℝ⁴ with the metric induced by the Euclidean inner product. A unit-speed geodesic from p with v⊥p and |v|=1 is γ(t)=cos(t)p+sin(t)v. It is closed with period 2π.

**Observer consequence.** The embedded ℝ⁴ formula is convenient but not the observer’s ambient vision. Intrinsic S³ sees along its geodesics; the formula supplies exact propagation. At time π every initial unit direction from p reaches −p. Thus the antipode has infinitely many geodesic directions from p, so a global inverse exponential map is impossible.

**Connection and frame.** Differentiate the formula in ℝ⁴ and project tangent to S³ to obtain intrinsic acceleration. Along a great circle, a tangent vector w orthogonal to both p and v is constant as an ambient vector and is parallel. The velocity γ̇ itself is parallel because γ is geodesic.

**Quotients.** Lens spaces and other spherical space forms quotient by finite isometry groups. A fundamental-domain wrap uses the group action in ℝ⁴ and its differential, not coordinate modulo arithmetic.

**Numerical boundary.** Normalize points and enforce p·v=0 after floating-point drift. Exact trigonometric propagation provides a baseline against which integration and frame code can be checked.
