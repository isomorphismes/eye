# Executable Eye reference cases

The original reference specification is now implemented by
[Acceptance.idric](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/Acceptance.idric),
[BoundaryChecks.idric](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/BoundaryChecks.idric),
[PrecisionChecks.idric](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/PrecisionChecks.idric)
and [CompanionChecks.idric](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/CompanionChecks.idric).
Run the [qualification entrypoint](../_/qualify.pi); its arguments, exact pins,
tolerances and remaining target boundaries are in
[QUALIFICATION.md](../docs/QUALIFICATION.md).

## Analytic propagation and transport

All production trajectories below execute Eye with Float32 scalar operations.

| Model | Initial point and velocity | Checked at t=0.5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E³ | p=(0,0,0), v=(1,1/2,1/4) | p=(1/2,1/4,1/8), constant v/frame |
| S³ | p=(0,0,0), v=(1/2,0,0) | x=tan(t/2), x'=1/(2 cos²(t/2)) |
| H³ | p=(0,0,1), v=(0,0,1) | z=z'=exp(t); transported x-axis components exp(t) |
| S²×ℝ | p=(0,0,0), v=(2/5,0,3/5) | x=tan(2t/5), z=3t/5 |
| H²×ℝ | p=(0,1,0), v=(0,4/5,3/5) | y=exp(4t/5), z=3t/5 |
| Nil | p=(0,0,0), v=(1,0,1) | x=sin(t), y=1−cos(t), z=3t/2−sin(2t)/4 |
| Sol | p=(0,0,0), v=(1,1,0) | p=(t,t,0), constant v |
| SL̃₂ | p=(0,1,0), v=(0,0,1) | p=(0,1,t), no angle wrap |

Each case checks velocity, speed and all six Gram entries as well as position.
The coupled SL̃₂ initial velocity (1,0,−1) is also compared at t=0.25 with
1,000 binary64 midpoint steps derived from independently differentiated
metrics, rather than from the implemented Christoffel functions.

H³ convergence compares requested step sizes 0.5 and 0.25 at time 1 against
exp(1). The finer component error must be below one quarter of the coarser
error and below 2e-5. Step-doubling remains a coordinate diagnostic, not a
certified intrinsic error estimate.

A length-4 S³ trace switches charts and passes through the pole omitted by its
original stereographic chart. Other chart tests check tangent, covector and
frame transformations, product-line preservation and round trips. Observer
motion transports facing.

## Torus and event coverage

Exact binary-fraction fixtures exercise positive and negative crossings on
all three axes, starts on either x face, resuming coordinate 1 while moving
inward, crossings exactly at the travel limit, repeated travel and simultaneous
corners. Simultaneous Z³ translations are resolved as a batch. A general
scene/quotient tie refuses arbitrary ordering. An unknown populated scene
exhausts refinement explicitly, and a nearer scene surface occludes a later
cube face.

The same repeated positive and negative torus trajectories execute with E4M3.
A separate E4M3 test shows positional stagnation refusing rather than silently
advancing its travel counter.

## Independent connection calculations

[IndependentConnection.idric](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/IndependentConnection.idric)
constructs metric matrices separately, takes central derivatives, inverts
the matrices and contracts the Levi-Civita formula. It compares every basis
pair and output component against Eye for each of the eight models.

[connection-inputs.tsv](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/connection-inputs.tsv)
and [symbolic-connections.pi](../examples/autogenerated/observation-qualification/symbolic-connections.pi)
reproduce the symbolic calculation with SymPy through the checked Ithon
frontend. The --corrupt-nil control must fail specifically at Nil component 0.
This symbolic test checks written expressions; the compiled numerical test
separately checks their implemented functions.

## Type and representation failures

The positive fiber/control fixture compiles. The unrelated-fiber fixture
must fail specifically on points 0.0 and 1.0; the two role fixtures must fail
on Tangent versus Cotangent. Unrelated CLI, package or import failures never
count as successful negative tests.

Precision cases include all 256 signed E4M3FN encodings, signed zero,
subnormals, ties-to-even, overflow and Float32 per-operation rounding.
Invalid domains and zero directions refuse. Logical ray packets round-trip
and reject wrong geometry. Their success is not a complete native ray/frame
ABI claim.

## Rendering and devices

The compiled host demonstration produces before/moved PPM images for E³,
the flat torus and H³, with a nearer occluding panel and metric-local lighting.
Magenta marks unresolved numerical/event decisions. A rendered pixel does
not prove a correct trajectory, a rigorous first-hit bound or a physical
device result. Direct native backend execution, A1 packaging, C67 packaging,
installation and physical behavior retain their separate unverified states.
